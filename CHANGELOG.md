# Changelog

Annotation guidelines evolve as edge cases surface. This log shows how the rules tightened over three rounds - the same way real annotation projects mature.

## v1.2
- Added ruling: `CRED RENT PAYMENT` and similar rent payments -> **Other** (rent is not in the label set and is not a plain person-to-person transfer).
- Added the tie-break priority order in Section 5 for items that fit two categories equally.

## v1.1
- Added ruling: instant-grocery delivery (`SWIGGY INSTAMART`) -> **Groceries**, not Food & Dining. Clarified that only restaurant/prepared-meal orders are Food & Dining.
- Added ruling: media subscriptions billed by a retailer (`AMAZON PRIME MEMBERSHIP`, `SPOTIFY INDIA`) -> **Entertainment**, not Shopping.
- Added Rule 2 ("categorise by what was bought, not who sold it").

## v1.0
- Initial 11-category label set.
- Base decision rules (merchant-first, direction of money, fees, last-resort "Other").
- First edge-case rulings: fuel -> Transport; refunds -> Income; pharmacy platforms -> Health.
